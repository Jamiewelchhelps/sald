About

Senior people, sharp opinions, zero drama.

Our story

Built by practitioners

Slade Technologies was founded by engineers and architects who'd seen the same patterns in too many places: half-finished platforms, slide decks instead of systems, and vendor armies that left when the contract did.

We started Slade to deliver the opposite — small senior teams that take ownership, ship to production, and leave clients better off than they found them.

Principles

How we make decisions

Six short rules we lean on when the path isn't obvious.

1

Outcomes over output

If the work doesn't move a metric the client cares about, we don't ship it.

2

Senior by default

Every pod is led by people who have done it before. No bait-and-switch staffing.

3

Small teams win

Four sharp engineers will out-ship fifteen mediocre ones. We staff for the work, not the invoice.

4

Bias to production

Real software in real environments. Demos in week two, not month two.

5

Leave it better

Documentation, runbooks, and training are part of the deliverable, not a stretch goal.

6

Tell the truth

Honest estimates. Honest reds and ambers. Honest invoices.
